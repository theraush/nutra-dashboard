Change #1: Stop fighting it with willpower

The problem

The slump arrives. You resist. You snack anyway. Then you feel bad, and promise to try harder tomorrow.

That loop runs every single afternoon. After a month it's not a bad day. It's a habit, 30 times over.

So the question isn't "how do I resist better?"

It's "what does my afternoon look like before the slump hits?"

Change #2: Rebuild the 90 minutes after lunch

This is the big one. Most slumps are set up between 12:30 and 2:00.

Step 1: Look at what you're doing now

A quick desk lunch. Then sitting, straight through. By 3 p.m. you're flat, and the pantry is ten steps away.

Step 2: Swap three things

Protein at lunch. Ten minutes of walking after it. A glass of water at 3. Small enough to do on a busy day.

That's Change #2. Now the afternoon isn't set up to fail.

NOW Change #3 makes 3 p.m. automatic:

Change #3: Give 3 p.m. a fixed routine

Decisions are where the snack wins. A fixed routine removes the decision.
